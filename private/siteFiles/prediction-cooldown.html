Prediction Cooldown

Making, editing, or deleting predictions is unavailable during the weekend period between 00:00:00 Saturday PST and 00:06:00 Monday PST. This is to ensure all predictions are processed smoothly and all issues are resolved before the next prediction period begins.

This cooldown is only present directly prior and during F1 race weekends. If you believe this message is being displayed incorrectly please contact support.
